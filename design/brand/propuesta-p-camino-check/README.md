## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one new original logo proposal for "Pruevia", a Mexican platform helping patients identify studies in medical orders, compare providers and prices, and choose where to get tested.
Design a custom uppercase P with a substantial solid deep teal #0F766E body and gently rounded corners. FIRST read must unmistakably be P. Its interior counter is a WHITE negative-space pathway: a simple open route flowing through the bowl and ending in a SMALL subtle integrated check at the bowl's right edge. The check must occupy no more than about 15 percent of the symbol, subordinate to the P, with a restrained soft green #65B586 accent only at the terminal tip. Make the route geometrically coherent with the P rather than adding a check badge on top. Preserve a recognizable vertical stem and rounded upper bowl. Two ideas only: P and path-to-choice.
Avoid plain typographic P with stuck-on check, giant checkmarks, symmetrical open-book/pages, leaves, political campaign emblems, shields, circles, pins, magnifiers, test tubes, nodes, medical crosses and decorative clutter.
Crisp flat vector-style design on fully opaque PURE WHITE background. No transparency, gradients, glow, textures, shadows or 3D.
Landscape presentation board showing ONE consistent proposal: large hero symbol above exact text "Pruevia" in bold friendly rounded sans serif dark navy #0F172A. Lower row includes the SAME symbol black on white and white on teal rounded app tile, maintaining identical geometry and a small thumbnail. Generous white space. No extra captions, numbers, slogans, stationery or device mockups. Quality original brand design; prioritize optical balance, thick enough features, and the check remaining a secondary subtle discovery.
